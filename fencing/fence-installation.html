New Fence Installation FAQs

How long does it take to install a new fence?

Most residential fence installations take a few days depending on the length of the fence line and site conditions. We'll give you a time estimate with your quote.

Do I need to know my property lines before you start?

It helps, but we can also assist in identifying likely boundaries, and for a legal property line survey, we may recommend a licensed surveyor for larger or disputed projects.

Can you also stain the fence once it's built?

Yes, many customers schedule staining a few weeks after installation once the wood has had time to acclimate.
