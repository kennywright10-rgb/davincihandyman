Fencing FAQs

What fence materials do you work with?

We repair, stain, and install wood privacy and picket fences, and can install other materials on request. If you're not sure what's right for your yard, we'll walk you through the options during your free estimate.

Do I need an HOA approval or permit for a new fence?

Many communities in Forsyth County and the surrounding suburbs require HOA approval before a new fence goes in, and some require a permit as well. We can help you figure out what your neighborhood requires before we start.

How long does a typical fence installation take?

Most residential fence installations are completed in a few days once materials are on site, depending on the length of the fence and the layout of your yard. Repairs and staining are usually finished in a single visit.
