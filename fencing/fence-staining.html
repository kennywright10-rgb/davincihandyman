Fence Staining in Cumming, GA

Georgia sun and humidity fade and dry out a wood fence faster than most homeowners expect, usually within 2-3 years. Staining isn't just cosmetic: it seals the wood against moisture and rot, which is what actually keeps a fence standing longer. We clean, prep, and stain the whole fence, not just the street-facing side.

Fence Staining FAQs

How often should a wood fence be stained?

Every 2-3 years for most homes in this climate, depending on how much direct sun and moisture the fence gets.

Do I need to be home during staining?

No, as long as we have access to the yard and fence line. We'll coordinate timing with you in advance.

Can you stain a new fence you didn't build?

Yes, we stain fences we install as well as existing fences built by others.
