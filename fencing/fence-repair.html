Fence Repair FAQs

Can you match the wood or style of my existing fence?

In most cases, yes. We'll do our best to match material, color, and style so the repair blends in rather than standing out.

Is it cheaper to repair or replace my fence?

If the posts and framing are sound, repair is almost always the more affordable option. We'll give you an honest read on which makes more sense for your situation.

Do you repair vinyl fences too, or just wood?

We work on wood and vinyl fences. We don't currently service chain-link.
