Both, we handle fence repair, staining, and new fence installation.

Every 2-3 years for most wood fences in Georgia's climate, depending on sun and moisture exposure.

Chimney Sweep

How often should my chimney be cleaned?

Most homes that use their fireplace regularly during the season should have it inspected annually and cleaned as needed based on creosote buildup.

How much does chimney cleaning cost?

Starting around $359 for a prefab chimney and $399 for brick masonry, depending on condition. We give a firm quote before any work starts.

How often should dryer vents be cleaned?

Once a year for most households, more often for larger families or homes with long vent runs.

How much does dryer vent cleaning cost?

Typically $229–$299, depending on the length of your vent run.

How often should air ducts be cleaned?

Every 3-5 years for most homes, sooner with pets, renovation dust, or allergy concerns.

How much does air duct cleaning cost?

Starting around $389 per system for up to 10 vents.
