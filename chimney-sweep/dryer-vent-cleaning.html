If your clothes are taking longer than usual to dry, the dryer feels hot to the touch, or you notice a burning smell, that's often a sign of lint buildup in the vent line, not the dryer itself. Dryer Vent Cleaning FAQs

How often should dryer vents be cleaned?

Once a year for most households, more often for larger families or homes with long vent runs.

Can I just clean it myself?

DIY kits reach the first few feet, but most buildup and blockages happen further down the line or at bends, which typically require professional equipment to clear fully.

Do you clean both the interior and exterior vent?

Yes, we clear the full line from the dryer connection to the exterior vent cap.

How much does dryer vent cleaning cost?
