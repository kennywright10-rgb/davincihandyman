Chimney Sweep FAQs

How often should my chimney be swept?

The general rule is once a year for a chimney in regular use, since creosote builds up with every fire and is the main cause of chimney fires. If you use your fireplace less often, we can help you decide whether an annual sweep is still the right interval.

Do you offer camera inspections?

Yes. We use a camera inspection tool to check the full length of the flue for creosote buildup, cracks, and other damage that isn't visible from the firebox, so you know exactly what's going on before any work starts.

Can I bundle dryer vent cleaning with a chimney sweep visit?

Yes — many customers have us handle both in the same visit. Clogged dryer vents are one of the most common causes of house fires, so it's an easy add-on while we're already on site.
