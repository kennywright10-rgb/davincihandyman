Chimney Cleaning FAQs

How often should my chimney be cleaned?

Most homes that use their fireplace regularly during the season should have it inspected annually and cleaned as needed based on creosote buildup.

How do I know if I need cleaning or repair?

Our inspection covers both, and if we find a structural issue during the cleaning, we'll tell you plainly and quote any repair separately before doing the work.

Do you clean gas fireplace chimneys too?

Yes, gas fireplace venting has its own inspection needs and we service both wood-burning and gas systems.

How much does chimney cleaning cost?

Cleaning and inspection is $299 for a prefab wood or gas-log fireplace, or $389 for a traditional brick masonry chimney.
