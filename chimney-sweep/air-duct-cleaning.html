Air Duct Cleaning FAQs

How often should air ducts be cleaned?

Every 3-5 years for most homes, sooner if you've had renovation work, pets, allergy concerns, or visible dust around vents.

Will this help with allergies?

Many customers notice an improvement, though results vary depending on the source of the allergy symptoms. It's one part of overall indoor air quality, not a guaranteed fix.

Can I bundle this with chimney or dryer vent cleaning?

Yes, many customers schedule air duct cleaning alongside a chimney sweep or dryer vent cleaning visit.

How much does air duct cleaning cost?

Starting around $389 per system for up to 10 vents. Larger systems are quoted individually. We'll confirm the exact price before any work starts.
