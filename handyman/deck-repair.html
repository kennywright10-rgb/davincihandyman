Deck Repair FAQs

How do I know if my deck needs repair or full replacement?

If the framing (posts, joists, ledger board) is sound and it's mostly surface boards or railings, repair usually makes sense. We'll tell you honestly if we think replacement is the smarter call rather than patching something that won't hold up.

Can you clean and restore an old, weathered deck?

Yes, deep cleaning and refinishing is one of our most requested deck services, as shown in the before/after photo above.

How long does a typical deck repair take?

Most board and railing repairs are completed in a single day. Larger structural repairs may take longer. We'll give you a time estimate with your quote.
