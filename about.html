About Davinci Home Improvements

Davinci Home Improvements is a family-owned handyman, fencing, and chimney sweep company based in Cumming, GA. For over 25 years we've been the crew local homeowners call for the jobs that need doing right the first time: from a leaky faucet to a new fence to a full chimney inspection.

We're still built the same way we started: a small, insured, background-checked team that shows up, does the job right, and treats your home like it matters. Our customers are the most important part of our business: most of our work comes from repeat customers and referrals, and we back it with no hidden fees.

How We Got Here

Davinci started small. One truck, one crew, and a simple idea: show up when you say you will, quote a fair price, and stand behind the work. Word got around Cumming the old-fashioned way, one referral at a time, and twenty-five-plus years later that's still how most of our business comes in.

Along the way we added fencing and chimney/venting work to the handyman services we started with, not because we wanted to be everything to everyone, but because customers kept asking the same crew they already trusted to handle the fence, the dryer vent, and the chimney too. We only added a service once we could do it to the same standard as the first one.

We're still a small, local operation on purpose. Every technician who comes to your home is insured, background-checked, and part of the same team, not a subcontractor pulled in for the day.

Our Approach

Quality Workmanship

We inspect the whole job, not just the spot you noticed, and fix what actually needs fixing, whether that's the quick version or the right version.

Transparent Pricing

You get a clear price before we start. No change orders that show up after the work is already underway.

Respect for Your Home

We clean up after ourselves, protect your floors and landscaping, and treat every property like it's the only job we have that day.

Community Roots

We're based in Cumming, we serve the neighborhoods around it, and most of our new customers are referred by someone we've already worked for.
